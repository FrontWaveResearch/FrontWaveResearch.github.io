A research instrument. Effective cost of debt is implied: reported FY finance cost divided by reported FY total debt (leases and all borrowings included), from exchange filings via market data providers. It can differ from a company's stated average cost — restructured or subsidised debt reads low, part-year drawdowns read high. Rankings are descriptive, not a credit opinion, an offer or investment advice. Searches run in your browser; nothing you type is stored or sent.
